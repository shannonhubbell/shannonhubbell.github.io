Join a discussion hosted by Catalight and Behavioral Health Business on autism care patterns across Europe and learn how clinicians and experts approach autism treatment in other countries.

Engage with Catalight’s Chief Clinical Officer Doreen Samelson, Ed.D., MSCP;  Psychiatrist Yvette Roke, MD, Ph.D., of GGz Centraal, Netherlands and Developer of the SAM app; and Alvin van Asselt, an autistic Ph.D. student and researcher, also of GGz Centraal, Netherlands.

Gain insights into the contrasting approaches to autism support in the United States and Europe—particularly the Netherlands, where the focus is shifting from high-hour Applied Behavioral Analysis (ABA) toward parent-mediated and self-directed care models.

Key takeaways include:

  • Progressive care that pinpoints the right measure and yields the most worthwhile outcomes
  • How the United States can incorporate European methods for more effective care and cost
  • Insights into parent-mediated care practices and their impact on patient and family wellbeing
  • The importance of incorporating autistic voices in treatment development and decision-making
  • Discussion on the ethical considerations of aiming to reduce autistic traits versus supporting individual autonomy
