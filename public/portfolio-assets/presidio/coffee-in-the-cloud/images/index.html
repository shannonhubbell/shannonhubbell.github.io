Industries

Manufacturing

Industry 4.0 is highly responsive, automated, and data-driven. IoT devices now blanket the factory floor, collecting information and monitoring operations—leading to AI-driven insights. These devices require ubiquitous connectivity across a modern, agile infrastructure so they can inform and improve increasingly complex operations.

Retail

Shoppers are returning in store while continuing to demand the convenience of ecommerce and touchless services—creating a need to deliver seamless experiences across digital and physical touchpoints. Retailers are now looking to create smart spaces through an investment in IoT and touchless technology—hoping to drive customer satisfaction, employee safety and ultimately prepare for future challenges.

Healthcare

Healthcare organizations face cyber and physical security concerns as well as the need to modernize patient and staff experiences with innovations like telehealth, hospital@home and smart hospitals. These challenges have opened the door for healthcare organizations to invest in cloud migration, smart hospital, cybersecurity and hybrid care initiatives.

Financial Services

The increase of digital banking has forced financial institutions to evaluate the role traditional banks play. In order for financial instructions to remain competitive and provide powerful customer and employee experiences, banks need to invest in improved connectivity and IoT devices within the branch without compromising the digital safety customers have come to expect.

Public Sector

The Federal government has disbursed an unprecedented amount of funds to help schools, states and localities invest in essential services like public and student safety, public health, human services, infrastructure and broadband. This has created a unique opportunity to invest in cloud migration, cybersecurity, bridging the digital divide, and smart city initiatives.
