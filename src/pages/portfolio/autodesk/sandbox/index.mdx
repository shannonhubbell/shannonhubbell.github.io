*"Autodesk and the American Society of Civil Engineers team up to inspire students at Abraham Lincoln High School in San Francisco to become makers. Through this partnership, students were provided the materials and direction to build their own Augmented Reality Sandbox."*

I was provided with nine hours of raw interview footage and b-roll and given free reign to edit it down to a tidy five minute narrative. This was a lot of fun to work on, partially because the educational initiative is so neat! I want to build and play around with one of these sandboxes!
