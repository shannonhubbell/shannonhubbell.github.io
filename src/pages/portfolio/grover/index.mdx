A finalist in Badass Digest's Filmmaking Frenzy competition, this is a trailer for the greatest movie never made. United States President Grover Cleveland is asked to come out of retirement to deal with a familiar threat: dinosaurs!

I wrote, directed, edited, and did the visual effects for this monstrosity in about two weeks. No dinosaurs were harmed.
