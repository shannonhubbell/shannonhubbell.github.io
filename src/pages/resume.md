# Resumé

I was born and raised in New Orleans, Louisana, but have lived in Oakland for the last twenty years. I love movies and libraries and dinosaurs.

## Skills

### Web

As a coder I have spent over 25 years building digital marketing material for clients such as **Adobe**, **Google**, **Autodesk**, **Microsoft**, and **Salesforce**. This has included full sites, campaign pages, EDM templates, animated banner ads, and virtual event applications.

HTML, Javascript, CSS, PHP, WordPress, Contentful, Astro, whatever tool seems appropriate for the task. I work from Photoshop, Figma, and GIMP.

More recently I have branched out into game development with Godot and Twine.

### Video

I have been working in video post-production for 30 years, in narrative and documentary film, as well as marketing videos. I use Adobe Premiere and After Effects.

### Writing

I write things. Screenplays, comics, film criticism. Other stuff. You can tell I'm a fancy writer because I sometimes use incomplete sentences in order to craft a casual tone in my resumé. You can find some of my film writing on [Lewton Bus](https://lewtonbus.net) and [CHUD](https://chud.com).
